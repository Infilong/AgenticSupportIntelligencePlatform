# Human review

Read the backend guide and the [review record](../../../../docs/plans/completed/m3-human-review.md).
- Preserve original drafts, citations and immutable decisions; final responses are separate.
- Bind decisions to workspace, run, draft identity and revision before scheduling continuation.
- Ordinary review is operator/admin; persisted policy-exception classification requires admin approval.
- Recheck requester, contributor and reviewer at fenced publication. Cancellation wins before publication.
- Approve/edit require current contextual evidence. Reject/clarify approve no answer and may
  address stale evidence. Questions become a next step only after fenced publication.
- Keep the immutable clarification question separate from subsequent customer-provided input.
- Development annotations are attributed inputs, not proof of automated semantic policy detection.
