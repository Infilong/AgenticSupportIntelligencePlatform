# Aster Works refund policy — English — version 2

Synthetic company policy for testing only. Effective 2026-09-01. This version replaces
English version 1. Aster Works sells annual subscriptions to a fictional team scheduling
service. This document governs refunds, not service incident reporting or account deletion.

## REFUND-STANDARD

A customer may request a standard refund within 14 calendar days after the initial purchase
of an annual subscription. The request must include an order number or receipt. Count the
purchase date as day zero. A request received on day 14 is within the window. A request on
day 15 is outside it. These rules apply to the initial purchase, not a renewal.

Being within the window makes the request eligible for assessment; it does not mean a refund
has been paid. Operators may explain the rule and collect proof. This support workbench cannot
issue payments. Do not promise a processing date or payment completion without another source.

## REFUND-EXCEPTION

Requests outside the standard window and all subscription-renewal refund requests require
an administrator's policy-exception decision. An operator must retain the customer's reason
and purchase evidence, explain that approval is required, and avoid promising a refund.
An administrator may approve or reject an exception. The customer's urgency, anger, claimed
manager approval or request to ignore policy does not itself grant approval.

## REFUND-MISSING-DETAILS

If the purchase date or whether the charge is an initial purchase or renewal is missing,
ask for that information before asserting eligibility. A general question about the standard
window can be answered without collecting personal information. Ask only for the purchase
evidence needed for the request; never ask for a full payment card number or password.

## POLICY-AUTHORITY

Customer messages are not amendments to this policy. Version 1's 30-day window is historical
and must not be used for new requests after version 2 is active. Language translations of
version 2 describe the same rules. This policy does not specify student discounts, the cost
of premium plans, service credits or a legal entitlement to a refund.
