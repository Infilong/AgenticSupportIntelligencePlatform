# Aster Works incident reporting — English — version 1

Synthetic company policy. Effective 2026-09-01. This document intentionally has no Japanese
or Chinese translation in the M0 fixture set. A Japanese or Chinese question may need this
English source; answer language is not a permission or source-language filter.

## INCIDENT-REPORT

Customers should report a suspected service outage within 24 hours after noticing it.
The report should include the affected feature, the approximate start time with time zone,
and whether the problem affects all users or only some users. An error code is useful if
available. A screenshot is optional; remove passwords, access tokens and personal customer
data before sharing one. Never request credentials to investigate a support incident.

## INCIDENT-BOUNDARY

The 24-hour period is a reporting guideline. It is not a promise of restoration within
24 hours, a response-time guarantee, an eligibility rule for compensation, or an instruction
to wait 24 hours before reporting. Operators must preserve these distinctions in responses.
This policy does not establish service credits or refund exceptions. Refer refund requests
to the refund policy and route exceptions for administrator review.
